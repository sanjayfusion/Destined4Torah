# Word study sources

Definitions are sourced by Strong’s number from James Strong’s Hebrew dictionary (1894) and Greek dictionary (1890), digitized and corrected by Open Scriptures.

- Hebrew: https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs/blob/master/hebrew/strongs-hebrew-dictionary.js (copyright 2010 Open Scriptures)
- Greek: https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs/blob/master/greek/strongs-greek-dictionary.js (copyright 2009 Open Scriptures)
- Both source headers state CC-BY-SA. Adapted dictionary data in `verifiedHebrew.ts` and `verifiedGreek.ts` remain CC-BY-SA. Definitions extracted verbatim apart from trimming whitespace. Lookup keys normalize spelling; this release adds 58 Hebrew and 78 Greek entries. Existing entries use source definitions for their recorded Strong’s number.
- Hebrew source credits David Instone-Brewer and David Troidl for e-text corrections; Greek source credits Ulrik Petersen for its XML preparation.

Word matching is a study aid, not a morphologically tagged Bible. Popups explicitly say “Possible dictionary match”; Hebrew recognizes limited attached prefixes, and Greek uses existing stem matching plus exact newly added lemma matches. No automatic claim about a word’s contextual meaning is made.

Sanjay’s teaching insight for light is paraphrased from his approved Genesis playlist teaching “Let there be Light”, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4boXYeN7J58 (connects Genesis 1:3 with John 1:1 and 7–10), and his supplied Exodus playlist teaching “Let there be Light in your Ark (Part 1)”, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKjpOAXZnb4, whose opening connects Genesis 1:3 with John 1:1–9. It is separately labeled and linked, not mixed into dictionary definitions. No generated commentary is attributed to Sanjay.
